Mail Cleaner

Clean up your Gmail inbox in minutes.

Mail Cleaner scans your Gmail and finds promotions, newsletters, spam and old one-time codes. You review the list, untick anything you want to keep, and the app moves the rest to Gmail's Trash. Nothing is deleted permanently.

Private by design

  • Emails are sorted on your phone. They are never sent to our servers.
  • The app only moves the emails you choose to Trash, where Gmail keeps them for 30 days.
  • No ads and no analytics.

Free and Pro

Mail Cleaner is free to download and use. Mail Cleaner Pro, sold through Google Play, adds unlimited cleaning, deeper scans, clean by sender, one-tap unsubscribe, multiple Gmail accounts and custom filters.
