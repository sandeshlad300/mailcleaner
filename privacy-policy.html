Mail Cleaner Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Short version: Mail Cleaner sorts your Gmail on your phone. We don't run servers that receive your email, we don't sell or share your data, and we never use it for advertising or to train AI models.

Who we are

Mail Cleaner ("the app") is published by Sandesh Lad. Contact: sandeshlad001@gmail.com.

Data the app accesses

With your permission, the app uses Google's Gmail API (scope gmail.modify) to:

  • read the sender, subject, date, labels, size, a short preview and mailing-list headers of the emails it scans;
  • move the emails you select to Gmail's Trash.

The app never sends email, never permanently deletes email and never changes your Gmail settings.

How the data is used and stored

  • Email data is processed only on your device to suggest which emails are clutter. It is kept in memory while the app is open and is not written to storage or sent to us or anyone else.
  • Gmail access tokens are kept in memory only.
  • The app stores, on your device only: the Gmail addresses you added, your settings, your Pro status and simple counters (how many emails you've cleaned). Uninstalling the app removes them.
  • When you tap Unsubscribe, the app contacts the unsubscribe address that the sender put in their own email. No other data is included.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are handled by Google Play. We receive no payment card details. Google's privacy policy applies to payments: policies.google.com/privacy.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your data with anyone. We have no ads and no analytics SDKs.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Mail Cleaner's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your choices

  • Remove an account in the app's Settings at any time.
  • Revoke the app's Gmail access at myaccount.google.com/connections.
  • Emails moved to Trash can be restored from Gmail for 30 days.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
